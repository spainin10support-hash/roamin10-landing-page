Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spain in 10 ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use our Android application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

1. Information We Collect

Spain in 10 is designed to be a lightweight travel guide. We do not collect, store, or process any personal data directly. Specifically:

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not store personal search data.
  • We do not collect cookies or track your location history.
  • We do not store any information you enter into the app.

2. Weather Data

The app displays weather information sourced via the OpenWeatherMap API. When weather is requested, a call is made to OpenWeatherMap's servers. We do not store or log this data. Please refer to OpenWeatherMap's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle data.

3. Third-Party Links & Affiliate Partners

Spain in 10 contains links to third-party websites and services, including:

  • Booking.com – hotel booking links (affiliate partner)
  • Amazon Associates – travel product recommendations
  • Travelpayouts partners – eSIM and car hire links
  • Viator – tour and activity links

When you click these links and make a purchase or booking, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We do not receive any personal data about you from these transactions. Each third party operates under their own privacy policy.

4. Google Play & Android

Spain in 10 is distributed via Google Play. Google may collect certain usage and diagnostic data as part of the Android platform and Google Play services. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details.

5. Children's Privacy

Spain in 10 is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request data deletion, please contact us at:
